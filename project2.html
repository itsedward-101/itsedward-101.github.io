What Keeps Netflix Shows in the Top 10?

Using OLS regression on 88,000+ weekly rankings to identify what actually drives content longevity ? and what doesn't.

88,021 observations
190+ countries
2 key drivers found

The Problem

Netflix invests billions in content production and marketing, but quickly needs to assess which titles have lasting viewer engagement. This project asked: what early performance indicators and content characteristics actually predict how long a title stays in the Top 10?

Understanding this helps Netflix make smarter decisions on content promotion, renewal, and budget allocation.

Dataset

Source
Kaggle -> Netflix Top 10 TV Shows & Films (weekly global rankings)
Records
88,021 observations
Coverage
190+ countries, multi-year weekly data
Dependent variable
Cumulative weeks in Top 10
Key predictors
Weekly rank, content category (TV/Film), language (English/Non-English), week number

Analytical Approach

Exploratory Analysis

  • Summarized average Top 10 weeks by country, type, and language
  • Created Tableau visualizations: histograms, bar charts, geographic maps, heatmaps
  • Key EDA finding: most titles lasted only 1?2 weeks; a small subset achieved 20+ weeks

Regression Model

  • Method: Multiple Linear Regression (OLS) using Python statsmodels
  • Goal: Quantify which attributes significantly drive longevity while controlling for other factors
  • Compared two time-encoding methods ? results were identical, confirming robustness

The Model

Cumulative Weeks in Top 10 = 0.14 + 0.03 * Week + 0.09 * Weekly Rank + 2.19 * Category_TV + 2.01 * Language_NonEnglish
R^2 0.107
88,021 Observations
2,639 F-statistic
< 0.001 p-value
p < 0.001 All predictors significant

What Actually Drives Longevity?

📺

TV Shows outlast films ? TV shows stay in the Top 10 approximately 2.19 weeks longer than films, controlling for all other factors.

🌍

Non-English titles outperform expectations ? Non-English content stays 2.01 weeks longer. The highest-performing segment: Non-English TV shows (avg 6.9 weeks vs 1.9 for English films).

📅

Release timing barely matters ? The week variable has a coefficient of only 0.03. When you release matters far less than what you release.

📈

Ranking helps slightly ? Higher weekly rank (closer to #1) adds marginally to longevity, but the effect is small compared to content type and language.

Strategic Recommendations

"Season Accelerator" Program

TV shows with early traction get: automatic greenlight for 1?3 extra episodes, prime homepage placement during peak hours, and auto-trailer playback on home screens.

Global Spotlight Pipeline

Fast-track dubbing for high-potential non-English titles ? from 21?30 days down to 7 days in 10+ languages. Create a dedicated "Global Spotlight" homepage row for international breakouts.

Reallocate Marketing Budget

Since timing doesn't drive longevity, reduce spending on "perfect release week" campaigns. Shift budget to content quality: stronger trailers, better thumbnails, global promotion for non-English TV.
